How MagicFlow Works

From event to resolution, MagicFlow orchestrates every step of the care coordination process.

01

Events Trigger

A referral arrives, a call completes, or a patient needs follow-up. MagicFlow listens for these events automatically.

02

Smart Routing

MagicFlow evaluates clinic-specific rules to determine the right workflow, assignment, and priority level.

03

Agent Orchestration

AI agents and staff work in parallel—automated calls, record gathering, scheduling—all coordinated seamlessly.

04

Real-time Tracking

Every action is visible. Every handoff is tracked. Nothing falls through the cracks.

AI Agents That Work Together

MagicFlow is the conductor. These agents are the orchestra.

Phone Agent (Lumi)

Makes outbound calls to gather patient history, confirm appointments, and handle refill requests—all with natural conversation.

  • Patient history intake
  • Appointment confirmation
  • Medication refill handling

Inbox Agent

Processes incoming referrals, extracts clinical data, and routes to appropriate workflows automatically.

  • Referral processing
  • Clinical data extraction
  • Smart workflow routing

Fax Agent

Sends and receives referral documentation, integrates with EHR systems, and tracks delivery confirmation.

  • Document transmission
  • EHR integration
  • Delivery tracking
